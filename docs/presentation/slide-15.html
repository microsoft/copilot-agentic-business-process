03 / High-level solution

Reference patterns worth repeating

Separate reasoning, durable process state and accountable human decisions.

01

Persist between stages

A Dataverse step event starts the next workflow.
No long-lived parent flow waits for a human.

02

Keep skills focused

Version business rules as focused skill packages.
Load the relevant instructions and knowledge.

03

Use explicit contracts

Fixed extraction JSON; field-level evaluation;
schema-bound SAP output with no invented fields.

04

Make decisions safe

Human gates, row-versioned claims and atomic completion preserve who decided what.

Reason with agents. Coordinate through persisted state. Keep people accountable.

Presenter notes & sources

These are patterns demonstrated by the reference, not a claim that the sample solves every reliability problem. Persisted state supports long waits and explicit recovery; it is not an exactly-once delivery or automatic retry guarantee. Skills are reviewable, versionable Markdown packages. Schema instructions guide generation but do not make model output infallible. Test business rules, output validation and recovery behavior before production.
